I am a Brazilian from Pará (real Amazon). I work with technology and am curious about many things, like learning and education, leadership, music, governance, sports, startups, behavior economy, and psychology. Currently, I am an Engineering Lead at Google, working on the counter-abuse for Egregious Harms Protections (CSAM, NCII, VE, etc.).

I build teams that build great products for startups and tech companies. For the past two decades, I worked for companies like [Nubank](https://www.linkedin.com/company/nubank/), [Amazon](https://www.linkedin.com/company/amazon/), [GetYourGuide](https://www.linkedin.com/company/getyourguide-ag/), [BHub](https://www.linkedin.com/company/bhub-ai/), [QEdu](https://www.linkedin.com/company/qedu/), [Tripda](https://www.linkedin.com/company/tripda/), [Dafiti](https://www.linkedin.com/company/dafiti/), [SiteApps](https://www.linkedin.com/company/siteapps/), among others. You can see it in detail on my [LinkedIn](https://www.linkedin.com/in/marcelioleal/).

Beyond that, I work as a consultant, auditor, and advisor for Startups, and I am an active member in some communities and events. Currently, I am a co-founder of Beyond Tech Conference and an advisor at [E-station](https://www.linkedin.com/company/e-station-energytech/).

I lived in 3 cities, and currently, I am in Vila Madalena and Pinheiros, São Paulo, most of the time (others are Belém-PA in Brazil, and Berlin in Germany). I am a kitesurfer, and from July you can see me in Ceará or Rio Grande do Norte, Brazil. I love deep conversations, life histories, traveling, and cultures.

Living in São Paulo put me in the middle of one of Brazil's most alive art scenes. Here I keep a running list of places I go back to — from street art to museums and galleries with rotating programs.

## Street art — where to walk

**Walking Vila Madalena's streets to see the graffiti is one of the most unmissable things to do in São Paulo.** No ticket, no schedule, and the city rewrites the walls constantly — pass the same wall three months later and there's new work on it.

- **[Beco do Batman](https://g.co/kgs/CCtZvAz)** — Vila Madalena. An open-air graffiti corridor that changes constantly. The classic when someone visits SP.

- **[Galeria Alma da Rua](https://www.instagram.com/galeriaalmadarua/)** ★ — Vila Madalena. Curated street art, right on the route between the bars and the cafés. Worth stepping in.

- **[A7MA](https://g.co/kgs/4AdSTvx)** ★ — Rua Medeiros de Albuquerque, 250, Vila Madalena. The "Seventh" is SP's reference gallery for street art: founded in 2012 by Enivo, Jerry Batista, Tché Ruggi and co. to democratize graffiti. They sell works and prints — a great place to take home a piece of São Paulo's scene.

- **Rua Augusta and surroundings** — Augusta/Consolação. More political and underground graffiti, more itinerant.

- **Pinheiros / Vila Beatriz** — The walls around Teodoro Sampaio and Fradique Coutinho concentrate work by artists like Kobra, Mundano, Cranio.

## Galleries I love

- **[Galeria Caboco](https://www.instagram.com/espacocaboco/)** ★ — Rua Navarro de Andrade, 89, Pinheiros. **In my opinion, an unmissable place.** It's my friend [Lair Uaracy](https://www.instagram.com/lairuaracy/)'s gallery — a lawyer turned visual artist and ceramicist, born in Belém do Pará (so it has a special meaning for me). The space is independent, small, intentionally built as a place for formation and experimentation. Their current program includes a beautiful project dedicated exclusively to women artists. If you like contemporary art with soul and without pretension, it's a must.

- **[Central Galeria](https://www.instagram.com/centralgaleria/)** — Rua Mourato Coelho, 751, Vila Madalena (near Inácio Pereira da Rocha). Strong Brazilian contemporary art program in a charming old house on Mourato. Pairs well with an afternoon walking the street.

- **[Instituto Tomie Ohtake](https://www.institutotomieohtake.org.br/)** — Pinheiros. My most frequent stop. Strong curatorial exhibitions, beautiful architecture, a good café to linger at.

- **[MASP](https://masp.org.br/)** — Avenida Paulista. The permanent collection is the best in Latin America. Always worth the ticket and an afternoon.

- **[Pinacoteca de São Paulo](https://pinacoteca.org.br/)** — Luz station. Two buildings (Pina Luz + Pina Estação), strong Brazilian collection, high-level temporary program.

- **[Museum of Image and Sound (MIS)](https://www.mis-sp.org.br/)** — Jardim Europa. Photography, cinema, music. Thematic exhibitions worth the trip.

- **[SESC Pompeia / SESC Pinheiros / SESC 24 de Maio](https://www.sescsp.org.br/)** — The SESC network runs exhibitions, concerts, and visual arts with an excellent program — often free.

- **[Galeria Fortes D'Aloia & Gabriel](https://fortesdaloiagabriel.com/)** — Pinheiros. Brazilian contemporary art.

- **[Galeria Luisa Strina](https://galerialuisastrina.com.br/)** — Jardins. One of the most traditional in SP.

- **[Galeria Jaqueline Martins](https://galeriajaquelinemartins.com.br/)** — Vila Buarque. Younger program, focus on contemporary Latin American art.

- **[Galeria Vermelho](https://www.galeriavermelho.com.br/)** — Vila Buarque. Contemporary art with experimental curation.

- **[Casa Triângulo](https://casatriangulo.com/)** — Jardins. Program with Brazilian and international artists.

## Framing

- **[Los Amigos Molduras](https://www.molduraslosamigos.com.br/)** — Rua Marquês de Itu, Vila Buarque (downtown). Where I get all my pieces framed. Fair price for the area, careful work, and they actually know how to handle original art (not shopping-mall framing).

---

If you're going to visit, worth calling ahead — several of these only run until mid-afternoon or close on Mondays. Commercial galleries generally open Mon–Fri on business hours and Saturday until noon.

I love coffee and, because of that, breakfast is the most important meal for me. I enjoy it slowly and I take my time to appreciate my coffee.

That passion made me visit some places and combine it with work. On this page I share the places I really recommend — either for a visit or to work a morning away.

## São Paulo <img class="state-flag" src="https://upload.wikimedia.org/wikipedia/commons/2/2b/Bandeira_do_estado_de_S%C3%A3o_Paulo.svg" alt="São Paulo state flag" width="22" height="16" loading="lazy">

**[Cupping](https://www.google.com/maps/place/Cupping+Caf%C3%A9/@-23.5547082,-46.6900533,18.87z/data=!4m6!3m5!1s0x94ce579634688e7b:0x1fda38b1e10d7651!8m2!3d-23.5549482!4d-46.6888214!16s%2Fg%2F11b7swl400?entry=ttu) — Vila Madalena**

My favorite coffee shop. Tables with outlets to work from and a nice menu. The bagel with egg spread and the yogurt with banana are my picks. Any Clever filter is superb. Always ask for a double. No internet.

**[Le Pain Quotidien](https://www.google.com/maps/place/Le+Pain+Quotidien/@-23.5549469,-46.6895265,18z/data=!4m14!1m7!3m6!1s0x94ce579634688e7b:0x1fda38b1e10d7651!2sCupping+Caf%C3%A9!8m2!3d-23.5549482!4d-46.6888214!16s%2Fg%2F11b7swl400!3m5!1s0x94ce579649f3c621:0xe34ea623b855c8ad!8m2!3d-23.5546254!4d-46.6887597!16s%2Fg%2F11gzrqdc7?entry=ttu) — Vila Madalena**

For the days you want to share a big table with 4+ friends and work together. The double espresso does it for me — the rest of the menu isn't my thing. They do lunch too.

**[Coffee Lab](https://www.google.com/maps/place/Coffee+Lab/@-23.5559731,-46.6939016,17z/data=!3m1!4b1!4m6!3m5!1s0x94ce57bd09c61f33:0x190b28e1510a77d0!8m2!3d-23.555978!4d-46.6913213!16s%2Fg%2F11h05dd8k?entry=ttu) — Vila Madalena**

Coffee Lab was the pioneer of good coffee in SP. It's an experience and also a beautiful place to bring someone for a long conversation about life. Workable, with internet.

## Coffee shops I miss

My tribute to spots I loved that are no longer around.

**[Livraria da Fox](https://www.oliberal.com/cultura/livraria-fox-se-despede-do-publico-apos-36-anos-funcionamento-1.640215) — Belém, PA** — Every kind of tapioca, but the "molhada" and the one with queijo cuia were unforgettable. I still remember the powdered Cappuccino that my ignorance let me love. Pará literature everywhere.

**[Aunt Benny](https://www.iheartberlin.de/2017/09/19/the-best-coffee-places-in-friedrichshain/) — Berlin** — Best Americano and best Avocado Toast I ever had. Rescued me on sad Sundays. Moved to Toronto, I think.

**[Workshop Cafe FiDi](https://www.google.com/maps/place/Workshop+Cafe+FiDi/@37.790703,-122.402119,3a,75y,90t/data=!3m8!1e2!3m6!1sAF1QipOBXXRVl3rYHncZBUI7bBUf_E06oXdwHjDox4gI!2e10!3e12!6shttps:%2F%2Flh5.googleusercontent.com%2Fp%2FAF1QipOBXXRVl3rYHncZBUI7bBUf_E06oXdwHjDox4gI%3Dw114-h86-k-no!7i4608!8i3456!4m11!1m2!2m1!1sworkshop+cafe!3m7!1s0x808580795532c085:0xca181db15a6404c!8m2!3d37.7906889!4d-122.4020428!10e5!15sCg13b3Jrc2hvcCBjYWZlWg8iDXdvcmtzaG9wIGNhZmWSAQ9jb3dvcmtpbmdfc3BhY2WaASRDaGREU1VoTk1HOW5TMFZKUTBGblNVUmpPVkJmTUROUlJSQULgAQA!16s%2Fg%2F12qfjbklj?entry=ttu) — San Francisco** — Many afternoons from there pretending I was Mark Zuckerberg just because I was in SF.

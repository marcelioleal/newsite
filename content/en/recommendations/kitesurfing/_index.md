Kite is a passion of mine — it's on the yearly goals list, every year.

It's also how I've met some of the most interesting people and places I know. On this page I leave recommendations for spots I've sailed.

Season rule of thumb: Brazil's Northeast runs **August to January** (trade winds from the east, consistent). The South (Floripa) peaks **November to March**. The Amazon (Pará) is seasonal and depends on the specific spot.

---

**Northeast — the classic Brazilian kiter pilgrimage**

- **São Miguel do Gostoso — Rio Grande do Norte** — Small town vibe, flat lagoon, consistent side-on winds. Great for progression without the crowd.

- **Icaraí de Amontada — Ceará** — One of the most beautiful lagoons in Brazil. Strong winds, more international crowd, hotel-kite-sunset routine.

- **Ilha do Guajiru — Ceará** — Huge lagoon, shallow water, pure freestyle. More remote, less touristy. Good for a kite-family combo.

- **Preá/Jericoacoara — Ceará** — The main hub. Mix of ocean and lagoons (Guriú, Lagoinha), strong winds, nightlife in Jeri.

- **Cumbuco — Ceará** — Huge beach, downwind run along the coast past dunes. Easy logistics (close to Fortaleza). Bigger crowds in peak season.

**Pará — Amazon kitesurfing, off the beaten path**

- **Salinópolis** — Seasonal winds from January to May on some beaches, warm water, full-on paraense atmosphere.

- **Fortalezinha/Algodoal** — Isolated island, boat-only access, strong wind outside rainy season. A rustic experience.

- **Santarém/Alter do Chão** — Wind on the Tapajós river. Unique conditions (fresh water, Amazon backdrop). Short window but worth it.

- **Mosqueiro** — River beach near Belém. Weekend kite with the local crew.

**Elsewhere in Brazil**

- **Atins — Maranhão** — Inside Lençóis Maranhenses. Difficult logistics, excellent wind, unique scenery (dunes + rain lagoons). Intermediate+.

- **Florianópolis — Santa Catarina** — East and south coast beaches on the island (Campeche, Armação, Guarda do Embaú). Opposite season from the Northeast — good if you want to kite year-round.

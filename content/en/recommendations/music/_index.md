## Playlists

### Break Beat Eletro

My playlist of breakbeat and melodic electronic music. 30+ tracks between broken beat and melodic house, with plenty of synth and chopped-up vocals.

**[Listen on Spotify ↗](https://open.spotify.com/user/marcelioleal)** *(direct playlist link coming soon)*

#### The vibe

Broken beat, emotional synths, 90s-rave nostalgia — but produced today. It's danceable but not heavy. More melody than punch, which makes it work both on the dancefloor and in headphones.

#### Core artists

- **BICEP**, the Belfast duo that anchors the playlist (Atlas, Just, Back 2 U)
- **Baril**, Dutch producer with three tracks (One More Rush, Vocales, Sense)
- **Apste**, also with three (Prisma, Ix, Soul Light)
- **Rampa**, from Keinemusik (2000, Terrace)
- **Sam Shure, Framewerk, AIKON and Marc Gonen**, two tracks each

Also along the way: DJ Marky & XRS, Maribou State, Télépopmusik, LP Giobbi and Jimi Jules.

#### When to listen

- Warming up before heading out
- Running or training
- Working, when the day needs both focus and energy
- On the road or late afternoon

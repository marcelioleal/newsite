Belém is the capital of Pará, in the Brazilian Amazon — and the city I grew up in. If you're going, here are some of my favourite spots.

**Restaurants**

- [Bliss](https://www.instagram.com/bliss.belem/)
- [Point do Açaí](https://www.instagram.com/pointdoacaibelem/)

**Tacacá** (the quintessential Amazonian soup — tucupi broth, jambu leaves, dried shrimp)

- [Tacacá do Leandro](https://www.instagram.com/tacacadorenatoficial/?hl=en)

**More food and drinks**

- [Amazon Beer](https://www.instagram.com/amazonbeer/?hl=en) — Great draft beer and a solid filhote (Amazon river fish) with Brazil-nut crust, served with tucupi rice.
- [Black Dog](https://www.instagram.com/blackdoghenglishpubb/) — Have a good draft beer and say hi to my friend Iuri.
- [Cosanostra](https://www.instagram.com/cosanostra_pub/) — Best carpaccio in the world. Great pizza and honest draft beer prices.
